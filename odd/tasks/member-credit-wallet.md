# Member credit wallet (saldo a favor)
Objective: one monetary wallet per member, deposited from the profile or from overpayment change, applied to any payment via a tick in Compra actual.
Branch: integration/frontend-design · Delivery: ask-on-risk · Runner: `pytest` (api), `vitest` (frontend)

## Specs
S1. "le gustaria que tambien existiera un saldo a favor" — one wallet per member (`members.credit_balance_minor`), separate from debts, never negative.
S2. "si una persona hace un pago de un producto y paga mas de lo que deberia, pues ese saldo se ponga a favor" — overpayment in cash can be left as saldo a favor instead of change (member assigned only).
S3. "en el perfil pues haya un boton para agregar saldo a favor" — profile button "Agregar saldo a favor" (cash deposit on an open shift).
S4. "al momento de pagar, pues haya como un tick o algo asi que diga, pagar con saldo a favor ... dentro del componente de Compra actual, pues eso se podria aplicar a cualquier pago dentro de la aplicacion" — tick in `CurrentPurchaseCard`, works for membership, product and rental payments, and debt abonos.
S5. "dentro de la vista de un cliente poder visualizar un balance de su deuda y saldo a favor" — member view shows debt balance and saldo a favor.
S6. "el saldo a favor no se tiene que incluir en los metodos de pago ... si tiene saldo a favor lo mencione como saldo a favor: tanto, y ahi aparezca el tick de usarlo, pero si no lo tiene pues no mostrarlo" — not a method tile; line "Saldo a favor: $X" + tick, hidden when balance is 0 or no member.
S7. (accounting, derived) Deposit = cash in drawer but liability, not sales. Application = income, no cash movement.

S8. Backend contract available after T1–T3 (all amounts integer centavos, `Idempotency-Key` header on POSTs):
- `POST /api/v1/registers/{register_id}/member-credit-deposits` body `{member_id, amount_minor}` → `MemberCreditMovement` (201, replay 200). Needs an open shift on that register; cash-only.
- `GET /api/v1/members/{member_id}/credit-movements?offset&limit` → `{total_items, items[]}`; item = `{id, member_id, branch_id, register_id, shift_id, kind: 'deposit'|'apply', amount_minor, balance_after_minor, order_id|null, debt_id|null, rental_charge_id|null, received_by, created_at}`.
- `POST .../membership-sales` and `.../membership-transfer-sales`: optional `credit_minor` (default 0); response gains `credit_applied_minor`; `payment.amount_minor` is only the cash/transfer part (0 when fully paid by credit; transfer cannot be fully credit → 409).
- `POST .../membership-debt-payments`: optional `credit_minor` ≤ `amount_minor` (the part of the abono paid from credit); receipt gains `credit_minor`.
- `POST .../product-sale-intent`, `.../product-sales`, `.../product-sales/resolve`, `.../product-sales/card`: optional `credit_minor` (same value on all four calls of one sale; requires `member_id`); response gains `credit_applied_minor`; `payment.amount_minor` = total − credit. Card: the Point intent amount must be `total − credit`; credit == total on the card endpoint → 409 (use the cash endpoint with `credit_minor == total`).
- Overdraft (credit > balance) → 409. Orders paid with credit cannot be returned (409, excluded from eligible returns).
- Overpayment "dejar como saldo a favor" needs NO sale-endpoint change: after the sale succeeds the client posts a deposit of the excess (own idempotency key).
- Member balance: `credit_balance_minor` is already returned by member reads (`financial.credit_balance_minor`, list items), also for receptionists. Shift summary `payment_methods` may now contain method `credit`, and `activities` family `credit`; payment history item method may be `credit`.
- Out of scope in this slice: rentals (lockers/gloves) and NewMemberRegistration do not accept credit → their `CurrentPurchaseCard` must not show the tick.
