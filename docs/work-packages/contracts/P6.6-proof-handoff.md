## 2. Detailed Evidence by Subpackage

### 2.1 P6.1 & P6.2 Contracts & Architectural Findings

The initial audit of the STAFF-1 baseline exposed critical architectural limitations and race boundaries, formally captured in **P6.1** and solved in **P6.2**:

1. **Gregorian 400-Year Recurrence Cycle Bounds:**
   The weekly recurrence generation algorithm was analyzed against the 400-year Gregorian cycle ($146,096$ days = $20,871$ full 7-day weeks). Mathematical verification confirms that recurrence expansions are strictly finite, day-of-week modulo arithmetic is time-invariant, and series lookups are bounded by calendar range clamping to prevent denial-of-service via unbounded expansion requests.

2. **Overnight Shift & DST Transition Matrix:**
   Work shifts crossing midnight (e.g., 22:00 to 06:00 next day) anchor their business date (`business_date`) strictly to the calendar day on which the shift began. IANA timezone calculations preserve wall-clock semantics during daylight saving "fold" and "gap" transitions without causing duplicate or dropped shifts.

3. **D-032 Branch Roster Independence:**
   Staff profiles (`StaffRosterProfile`) represent branch-bound operational labor entities. They require no user accounts, system logins, or global identity unification. Cross-branch deduplication and profile overlap checks are strictly prohibited.

4. **D-033 Dual-Timestamp Clock Integrity:**
   Client wall clocks are inherently untrusted (subject to network latency, drift, or local manipulation). All check-in payloads mandate both `client_recorded_at` and `server_recorded_at`.

### 2.2 P6.3 Backend Implementation (`api/` — Commit `8c66ff2`)

The backend implementation delivered under commit `8c66ff2` transitioned the data model and API surface from flat daily records to occurrence-linked attendance:

1. **Occurrence-Linked Schema & Multi-Shift Support:**
   `StaffAttendanceRecord` was updated with:
   - `series_id` (UUID foreign key referencing `StaffScheduleSeries`, nullable for unscheduled / ad-hoc shifts).
   - `occurrence_date` (local calendar date).
   - `scheduled_start_time`, `scheduled_end_time`, and `tolerance_minutes`.
   - `client_recorded_at` (UTC timestamp claimed by client).
   - `review_required` (boolean flag) and `review_reason` (string explanation).
   - `idempotency_key` (UUIDv4) and `payload_hash` (SHA-256 fingerprint).
   
   The database unique constraint was restructured from `(branch_id, profile_id, business_date)` to allow multiple shifts per employee on the same business date across distinct schedule series or ad-hoc entries.

2. **Occurrence Tolerance & Status Evaluation:**
   Check-in timing is dynamically evaluated against the active schedule rule or single-date `StaffScheduleException` (`override`):
   $$\text{Lateness Threshold} = \text{scheduled\_start\_time} + \text{tolerance\_minutes}$$
   - Check-in before threshold $\rightarrow$ `status = "present"`
   - Check-in after threshold $\rightarrow$ `status = "late"`
   - Unscheduled check-in $\rightarrow$ `status = "present"`, `series_id = NULL`

3. **D-033 Clock Skew Enforcement:**
   $$\Delta t = |\text{server\_recorded\_at} - \text{client\_recorded\_at}|$$
   When $\Delta t > 300\text{ seconds}$ ($5\text{ minutes}$), the record is persisted with `review_required = True` and `review_reason = "CLOCK_SKEW_EXCEEDED"`. Under no circumstances is the client timestamp discarded or fabricated.

4. **Strict Idempotency & Replay:**
   Requests submitting an identical `idempotency_key` and matching payload hash return the previously committed record with HTTP `200 OK` (idempotent replay). Requests reusing a key with altered payload attributes fail with HTTP `409 Conflict`.

5. **Append-Only Corrections & RBAC:**
   Introduced the `StaffAttendanceCorrection` entity:
   - Modifications never mutate historical check-in or checkout records in place.
   - Every adjustment appends a new audit record capturing `previous_status`, `corrected_status`, `correction_reason`, `corrected_by`, and timestamp.
   - **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Users with role `receptionist` are strictly bounded to their assigned `branch_id`. Global administrators (`admin`) retain cross-branch correction authority.

### 2.3 P6.7 / C-16 Architectural Decoupling (Commit `59444d6`)

Contract **C-16** resolved the legacy circular dependency recorded between **BK-029** (Member Check-in) and **BK-031** (Staff Attendance) in `odd/tasks/backend-implementation.md`.

1. **Formal Proof of Zero Runtime Coupling:**
   - **Member Check-in Access Control (P3):** Governed by customer contracts, membership periods (`[starts_on, ends_on)`), entitlement quotas, debt thresholds (**D-013**), and branch permissions.
   - **Staff Labor Attendance (P6):** Governed by employee rosters, weekly shift recurrence, occurrence tolerances, and supervisor auditing.
   - **Turnstile Invariant:** Physical barriers (turnstiles/speed-gates) actuate solely based on customer entitlement evaluation. A receptionist’s absence, delayed check-in, or shift gap **never** inhibits member entry.
