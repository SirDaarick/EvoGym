# Business Rules and Decision Register

**Rule:** a documented behavior is not automatically an approved production rule. A `Confirmed/documented` row records current requirements, not implementation. An `Unresolved` row blocks the dependent behavior until the owner decides. The master ledger remains the only BK progress source.

**P1 implementation note:** BK-012 supplies a minimal audit append primitive only. It does not resolve audit retention/access, nor does it claim every business operation is automatically audited; unresolved policy and implementation gates remain with their owning tasks.

## Contradictions and interpretation guardrails

- A prototype may couple payment completion and activation, while the database map says they are separate. Payment settlement and membership activation remain separate state transitions (D-005, R-001); confirmed payment triggers activation.
- “Paid” must not collapse authorized, pending, confirmed, allocated, settled and refunded states.
- “Inventory count” must not collapse observation and adjustment.
- “Expiry” must not collapse rental return; “return” must not collapse refund.
- UI totals do not define taxes, fee treatment, rounding, or accounting categories.
- Point `processed` and Checkout Pro `processed` are provider-channel statuses; each must also satisfy its channel-specific account, status detail, amount and currency verification before internal settlement.
